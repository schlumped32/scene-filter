Scene Filter for Mac

1. Drag "Scene Filter" into your Applications folder.
2. Double-click it. macOS says it can't verify the app: click Done.
3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, click "Open Anyway" next to Scene Filter, and enter your password.
   (macOS 14 or older: right-click the app, choose Open, then Open.)
4. The first launch sets itself up: a few minutes, needs internet, about 700 MB of space.
   Then it opens in your browser. Drag a movie into the drop area, or click "Choose movie..." (quicker for big files).

If macOS says the app "is damaged", open Terminal and run:
  xattr -cr "/Applications/Scene Filter.app"
then open it again.

A small "Scene Filter is open in your browser" window stays up while it runs. Use it to reopen the page or quit.
Filtered movies and timestamp lists are saved in your Movies/Scene Filter folder.
