## Scene Filter, version 8

### A fresh look
- **Sage & Forest:** a soft sage-green design in light mode, and deep forest green in dark mode. It follows your computer's light or dark setting on its own.
- **A simpler screen in three steps:** **Movie** (choose it, then scan), **Review** (see what was flagged and untick anything you want left alone), and **Save** (cut out, blur, black screen, or just the times).
- Plainer wording throughout, and the detection settings are tucked away until you need them.

Nothing about scanning or saving changed. This update installs itself the next time you open Scene Filter.
