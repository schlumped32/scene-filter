## Scene Filter, version 9

### New
- **Light/dark switch:** a sun/moon button at the top right. Scene Filter follows your computer's setting until you click it, then remembers your choice.
- **Look closer:** each flagged scene now has a small blurred preview and a **Look closer** button. It opens a larger, lightly blurred view of that scene only, enough to tell what kind of scene it is, so you can decide whether to filter it. **Blur again** closes it. Previews are never shown clear.

### Removed
- The "Show unblurred preview images" setting, replaced by Look closer.

This update installs itself the next time you open Scene Filter.
